Dead Air
America

A young journalist's account of media, politics, and the erosion of trust in modern America.

Dead Air America turns a critical eye on modern America — its newsrooms, its institutions, its classrooms, and its politics — and asks what happens to a country when the signal of honest information gets buried under noise.

It is not a partisan argument for one side of the aisle. It is a close look at how trust breaks down between citizens and the institutions meant to inform them, and what rebuilding that trust might require from media, government, and readers alike.

Excerpts, press materials, and the release date will be shared here as soon as they're set. Members of the press can request review copies through the contact page.

Questions

What books has Luke H. Johnson written?

Luke H. Johnson has written two published nonfiction books, We're All the Same and Becoming You: Advice From a Teenager, and is completing a third, Dead Air America, a work of political nonfiction.

What is Dead Air America about?

Dead Air America examines modern America through the eyes of a young journalist, looking at media, political institutions, education, and technology, and asking how much trust still holds those systems together. It is a work of political nonfiction and media criticism, not a partisan argument for one side of the aisle.

What is We're All the Same about?

We're All the Same focuses on autism awareness, empathy, and the idea that every person deserves to be understood and included.

What is Becoming You about?

Becoming You: Advice From a Teenager is a book of advice from a teenager about personal growth, identity, confidence, and resilience.
