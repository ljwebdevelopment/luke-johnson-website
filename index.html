Upcoming Book · Political Nonfiction

Dead Air America

Notes on a country divided, distracted, and losing the signal.

Dead Air America is Luke Johnson's upcoming nonfiction book about growing up in a country where trust in institutions — media, politics, education, and technology — already feels fractured. Written from the perspective of a young American watching these systems shape his generation in real time, the book asks what it means to find a voice in a country that often feels like it has stopped listening.

About Luke

An Oklahoma writer speaking from inside the questions his generation is living.

Luke Johnson is an author, columnist, and speaker from Tahlequah, Oklahoma. Through his books, columns, and public discussions, he uses his perspective as a young writer to speak honestly about the world his generation is inheriting — rooted in Oklahoma, reaching readers through newspapers and media outlets across the country.
