The new book · Political nonfiction

Dead Air
America

Notes on a country divided, distracted, and losing the signal.

Dead Air America is about growing up in a country where trust in institutions — media, politics, education, and technology — already feels fractured. Written from inside a generation watching those systems shape it in real time, the book asks what it means to find a voice in a country that often feels like it has stopped listening.
