Upcoming Book · Political Nonfiction

Dead Air America

Notes on a country divided, distracted, and losing the signal.

Dead Air America is Luke Johnson's upcoming nonfiction book about growing up in a country where trust in institutions — media, politics, education, and technology — already feels fractured. Written from the perspective of a young American watching these systems shape his generation in real time, the book explores the noise, pressure, and confusion of modern life while asking what it means to find a voice in a country that often feels like it has stopped listening.

As a work of political nonfiction and media criticism, Dead Air America examines American institutions, civic discourse, and modern culture through the perspective of a Gen Z political author — part of a broader conversation about journalism, trust, and the future of the country.

About Luke

An Oklahoma writer speaking from inside the questions his generation is living.

Luke Johnson is an author, columnist, and speaker from Tahlequah, Oklahoma. Through his books, columns, and public discussions, he uses his perspective as a young writer to speak honestly about the world his generation is inheriting.

His writing focuses on technology, education, culture, politics, and the issues facing young people today — rooted in Oklahoma and reaching readers through newspapers and media outlets across the country. Beyond writing, Luke is also an entrepreneur and website developer, building and maintaining the digital platforms — including this site — that carry his work to readers.
